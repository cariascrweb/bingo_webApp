Tap a square to type your card number. Each column only accepts its own range (B 1–15, I 16–30, N 31–45, G 46–60, O 61–75) — invalid or repeated numbers are rejected. The centre is a free space. Squares fill in on their own as numbers are called.

Mark cells on the blank card below to define a winning pattern (e.g. the four corners), then save it as a goal. You can save more than one — you'll get an alert the moment any of your cards completes a saved pattern.
